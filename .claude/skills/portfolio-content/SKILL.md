# portfolio-content Skill

## When it applies
This skill applies to all content-related tasks for Siva Varman's personal portfolio website including:
- Writing or editing biographical information ("About Me" sections)
- Creating project descriptions and case studies
- Writing blog posts or technical articles
- Crafting skill summaries and experience descriptions
- Creating content for navigation, headers, footers
- Writing metadata (page titles, descriptions)
- Creating call-to-action text or motivational content
- Editing any textual content that appears on the portfolio site

## Mandatory rules
1. Focus on clarity, precision, and professional tone appropriate for a technical portfolio
2. Highlight Siva's technical expertise, accomplishments, and unique value proposition
3. Use active voice and strong action verbs when describing skills and experiences
4. Include specific, quantifiable achievements where possible (e.g., "Improved performance by 40%")
5. Maintain consistency in tense, tone, and style throughout all content
6. Optimize for readability with clear headings, short paragraphs, and scannable formatting
7. Include relevant technical keywords naturally (for SEO and clarity)
8. Show, don't just tell - provide concrete examples of work and impact
9. Address the target audience (technical recruiters, fellow developers, potential clients)
10. Keep content concise while being comprehensive - respect the reader's time

## Prohibited actions
1. Do NOT use generic, filler content or placeholder text (Lorem Ipsum, etc.)
2. Do NOT exaggerate or misrepresent skills, experience, or accomplishments
3. Do NOT use overly casual or unprofessional tone inappropriate for a technical portfolio
4. Do NOT create content that is irrelevant to Siva's professional brand or target audience
5. Do NOT use marketing buzzwords or empty phrases without substantive meaning
6. Do NOT neglect proofreading for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors
7. Do NOT make content overly verbose or difficult to scan quickly
8. Do NOT forget to update content when skills, projects, or experience change
9. Do NOT use first-person perspective inconsistently (choose and stick to one style)
10. Do NOT include personal information that should remain private (address, phone, etc.)
